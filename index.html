Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026 · App: Mystic Rituals

For entertainment purposes only. Mystic Rituals is an entertainment experience. Rituals, spells and messages do not produce real physical, medical, financial or legal effects and are not a substitute for professional advice.

1. Who we are

Mystic Rituals is operated by Leanid Navumau (“we”, “us”). Contact: lnv@anche.no.

2. What we collect

  • Name or nickname that you enter when ordering a ritual — used solely to personalize the ritual performed for you.
  • Gender (optional) — used solely to personalize the wording of the ritual. You may select “Prefer not to say”.
  • Order details — which ritual you ordered and when.
  • Purchase information — purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We receive only a transaction identifier to verify your purchase.
  • Random device identifier — a random code generated on your device to link your orders to their results. It is not an advertising identifier and is not used for tracking.

We do not collect your real identity documents, address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or health data. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. How your data is used and shared

Your name/nickname, optional gender and ritual order are shared only with the practitioner who performs your ritual. To deliver the order to the practitioner, the information is transmitted through the operator’s messaging channel (Telegram), which acts as a technical delivery service. Order records are stored on our hosting provider’s servers.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Retention

Order records (including the name you provided) are kept for up to 90 days after delivery, after which they are deleted, unless a longer period is required by law.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing lnv@anche.no with the subject “Data request” and including the device ID shown in the app under Settings → Privacy. We respond within 30 days. Deleting the app does not delete server records automatically — please send a deletion request.

6. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and ritual purchases are intended for users aged 18 and over.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We apply the principle of data minimization: we collect only what is needed to perform the service you ordered.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with a new effective date.
